1. Introduction

QCS ("we," "us," "our") operates the QCS Trust & Status Center at info.qcloud.systems ("this site"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you visit this site and how it is handled. It applies to this site only. Other QCS products and services may have their own privacy policies.

In short: this site has no accounts, no forms, no cookies, no analytics, and no advertising. We do not collect personal information about visitors beyond the basic technical records any website host keeps.

2. Information We Collect

  • Hosting logs: like any website, this site is served by a hosting provider that records basic technical information about each request, such as IP address, browser type, the page requested, and the time. These records are used to deliver the site and keep it secure.
  • Nothing else: this site does not set cookies, does not use browser storage, does not run analytics or tracking scripts, and has no sign-up, login, or contact forms.

3. Requests Your Browser Makes to Other Services

Some parts of this site are loaded by your browser directly from other services:

  • Service status: the status section fetches each provider's public status feed directly from that provider. Those providers receive the standard technical information your browser sends with any request, such as your IP address.
  • Fonts: the site's typeface is loaded from a third-party web font service, which receives the same standard technical information.

These requests carry no information about you from this site. Each service handles the requests it receives under its own privacy policy.

4. How Information Is Used

Hosting logs are used only to:

  • Deliver this site and keep it available
  • Detect and prevent abuse, attacks, and other malicious activity
  • Comply with legal obligations

5. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade information about visitors, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling. We may disclose information only when required by law or valid legal process, or to protect the security of this site and its visitors.

6. Security Reports

If you report a vulnerability through the process in our Security Policy, the details you choose to send are used only to investigate and fix the issue and, with your permission, to credit you. Reports are submitted through a third-party platform, whose own privacy policy applies to your account there.

7. Data Retention

We do not keep a database of visitor information. Hosting logs are retained by the hosting provider for a limited period under its own retention practices, and are then deleted.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information, such as the right to access or delete it. Because this site does not collect personal information beyond hosting logs, there is usually nothing for us to provide or delete. If you have a question or request, contact us using the details below and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Third-Party Links

This site links to other websites. This Privacy Policy applies only to this site. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of other websites.

10. Children's Privacy

This site is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date, and significant changes will be announced on the Notices page.
