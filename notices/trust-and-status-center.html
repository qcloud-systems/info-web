Introducing the QCS Trust & Status Center

info.qcloud.systems is now the QCS Trust & Status Center: one place to check the health of the services QCS depends on, read notices about changes and incidents, and review the policies that govern how QCS handles data and security.

What changed

  • Notices are a new section for incident reports, maintenance windows, security advisories, and service changes. Each notice is dated, categorized, and available as an RSS feed.
  • Policies — the Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, Security Policy, and Accessibility Statement — are grouped in one section.
  • Engineering articles and general documentation are no longer published here.

What stays the same

  • The site address is unchanged, so existing links to the Privacy Policy and Security Policy keep working.
  • Upstream service status, What's New, and the roadmap continue as before.
  • Security reports are still received privately through the process in the Security Policy.

Staying informed

Subscribe to the notices RSS feed to hear about incidents and changes as they are published.
